Portfolio adaptation: original mesh parts were joined and simplified, geometry was
meshopt-compressed and the four original texture maps were resized and converted
to WebP. The viewer sets the vehicle length to 8.5 m. The snowfield and the
separately modeled inhabited interior are additions to the scene.

Relay 23 crew refit: the viewer applies a snow-dust material treatment to
the lower running gear. A separately authored Blender field kit adds the
service scoop and its mounts, snow displaced by the tracks and glazing
detail. The original hull geometry and paint maps retain the credit above.
